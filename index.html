Questions

FAQ

How do I book a car?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates, the vehicle you need, and whether you want a driver. We'll reply with availability and a quote.

Can I take the car without a driver?

Yes — self-drive (lepas kunci) is one of our rental options. Tell us your plans and we'll confirm the terms on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver to the airport or my hotel?

Yes, we offer both airport delivery and hotel delivery. Confirm your pick-up point with us on WhatsApp when booking.

What type of cars do you have?

Seven categories: Brio/Agya, Avanza/Xenia, Suzuki XL7, Innova Reborn, Innova Zenix, New Alphard, and Hiace Premio/Luxury.

How do I get a rental quote?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with the car type, dates, and pickup location. We'll reply with the price and any terms you need to know.
